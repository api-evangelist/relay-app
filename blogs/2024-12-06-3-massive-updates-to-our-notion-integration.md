We’ve just launched three major updates to our Notion integration! You can now read existing Notion Page contents and use them in automations, enjoy native support for Notion’s Webhook Actions for easier and faster workflows, and upgrade to instant Notion triggers for real-time automation. These features make connecting Notion to AI tools and other apps more powerful than ever.
