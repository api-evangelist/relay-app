This guide demonstrates how to automate invoice processing using Relay.app through four phases: capture, extraction, approval, and recording. The workflow leverages AI to extract structured data from invoices in various formats while maintaining human oversight before sending information to accounting systems like QuickBooks or Xero. The approach reduces manual data entry while preserving approval checkpoints for accuracy and compliance.
